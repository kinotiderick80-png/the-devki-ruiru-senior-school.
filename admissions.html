How to Apply

Four simple steps to your bright future.

1

Submit an Enquiry

Complete our WhatsApp enquiry or email us. We are excited to hear from you and will respond within 24 hours!

2

Collect the Application Pack

Pick up the application form and prospectus at the school office. Our friendly staff will guide you through every detail.

3

Interview & Assessment

Meet our team for a friendly interview and academic assessment. This is a chance for us to get to know you — no pressure, just a conversation!

4

Offer & Enrollment

Receive your offer letter and complete enrollment for 2026. Welcome to the family! 🎓

What to Bring

  • KCPE / Junior School results slip
  • Birth certificate copy
  • Two passport photos
  • Leaving certificate (if applicable)
  • Medical form
