Welcome

Where excellence and scholarship rise together.

At The Devki Ruiru Senior School we cultivate confident, God-fearing young women and men. Our teachers, chaplains and mentors guide every learner to discover their gifts and use them for the good of family, nation and the world.

From rigorous academics to service, sports and the arts — every day here is a chance to grow in wisdom, courage and grace.

Message from the Board of Management

Together, Let Us Ignite the Future

On behalf of the Board of Management, I am delighted to welcome you to our school website. Our school is committed to providing a supportive, disciplined, and inspiring learning environment where every learner is given an opportunity to discover and develop their unique talents, skills, and abilities.

The Board of Management works closely with the school administration, teachers, parents, learners, and the wider community to promote quality education and ensure the school continues to grow as a centre of excellence.

We believe that education is a shared responsibility. Through teamwork, discipline, innovation, integrity, and commitment, we aim to equip our learners with the knowledge, values, competencies, and confidence they need to succeed in an ever-changing world.

Joseph Murumuri

Chairman, Board of Management

Principal's Remarks

Karibu to The Devki Ruiru Senior School

It gives me great pleasure to welcome you to The Devki Ruiru Senior School, a place where academic excellence meets character, discipline, and purpose.

Our school was founded on the belief that every student has unique potential. We nurture that potential through rigorous academics, talent development, and strong values of integrity, respect, and servant leadership.

As we move forward, our goal remains clear — to raise scholars, leaders, and patriots who will make a positive difference in Kenya and the world.

Deputy Principal's Remarks

Together We Build Excellence

As the Deputy Principal of The Devki Ruiru Senior School, I am honoured to serve this remarkable institution and the extraordinary young people who walk through our gates each day.

Our students are well disciplined, motivated, and ambitious. As we embark on the CBE Curriculum, we have put in place programmes to support learner-centered learning, ensuring we produce well skilled individuals.

Our school is very well staffed across all departments, with teachers fully accredited by the TSC. This ensures consistency and stability for our learners.
