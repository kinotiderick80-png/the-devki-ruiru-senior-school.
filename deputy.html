Deputy Principal's Remarks

Together We Build Excellence

"Welcome to The Devki Ruiru Senior School."

Welcome to The Devki Ruiru Senior School. The institution offers a very broad curriculum designed to satisfy the new CBE Curriculum. There are 24 subjects spread across the 3 pathways.

Our Curriculum at a Glance

In addition to the 6 mandatory subjects (Mathematics, English, Kiswahili, CSL, PE and ICT), the subjects are categorised under:

We are proud to say that our students are well disciplined. They are motivated and ambitious. As we embark on the CBE Curriculum and embrace Grade 10s, we have put in place programmes to support learner-centered learning. Indeed, we are sure to produce well skilled individuals at the end of their course here.

The school is very well staffed in all the departments. The greatest advantage being that our team of teachers are all graduates accredited by the TSC. This means that teacher turnover rate is negligible. In turn, this ensures consistency and stability for our learners.

We are open to welcome more students who would want us to help walk the journey of discovery as they acquire skills and education.

— Madam Munangwe, Deputy Principal

The Deputy Principal's Key Responsibilities

Five pillars of administration that ensure the smooth running and academic success of our school.

📜

Curriculum Delivery

Overseeing the implementation of all 24 CBE subjects across STEM, Social Sciences, and Arts & Sports pathways.

🛡

Student Discipline

Maintaining a culture of respect, order, and self-regulation through fair policies and guidance programmes.

👥

Staff Coordination

Ensuring effective deployment of TSC-accredited teachers and fostering collaborative teamwork.

💡

CBE Implementation

Leading the transition to Competency-Based Education through learner-centred programmes and assessment.

🤝

Parent Partnership

Building strong bridges between school and home through engagement and open communication.

🏆

Skills Development

Ensuring every student acquires practical skills for higher education and the world of work.
