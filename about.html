Our Story

A community shaped by purpose.

The Devki Senior Secondary School was established to walk with young Kenyans through the most formative years of their journey. In every classroom, chapel service, sports field and community activity, we nurture learners into confident, God-fearing leaders ready for university, work and life.

Our motto, Ignite Your Future, is more than words — it is a promise. We commit to fan every learner's spark until it becomes a steady, guiding light.
