Principal's Remarks

Karibu to The Devki Ruiru Senior School

"It is with great joy that I welcome you to our school family."

It gives me great pleasure to welcome you to The Devki Ruiru Senior School, a place where academic excellence meets character, discipline, and purpose. As the Principal, I consider it both a privilege and a profound responsibility to lead this institution in shaping the minds and hearts of Kenya's next generation of leaders.

Our school was founded on the unwavering belief that every student possesses unique potential — potential that, when properly nurtured through quality education, mentorship, and spiritual guidance, blossoms into remarkable achievement. Our responsibility as educators and mentors is to identify that potential, cultivate it with care and rigour, and prepare young men and women who are not only competent in the classroom, but also confident, principled, and ready to serve the nation with honour.

Academically, we are privileged to offer the three CBE Senior School pathways — Arts & Sports Science, Social Sciences, and Science, Technology, Engineering & Mathematics (STEM) — through a vigorous and supportive learning environment. Our dedicated teaching staff, all accredited by the Teachers Service Commission (TSC), work tirelessly to ensure that every learner is prepared to excel in national examinations and beyond. We maintain small class sizes to guarantee individual attention, and our modern laboratories, library, and digital learning resources provide the tools necessary for 21st-century education.

In talent and skills development, we encourage robust participation across language clubs, mathematics competitions, social subjects symposiums, competitive sports, performing arts, technology and innovation hubs, science fairs, and entrepreneurial initiatives. We believe that education extends far beyond textbooks. Every student deserves the opportunity to discover, develop, and deploy their unique gifts for the benefit of society.

In character formation, we uphold the timeless values of integrity, respect, hard work, discipline, and servant leadership — values that will guide our students long after they graduate from our gates. Our morning devotions, guidance and counselling programmes, and community service initiatives ensure that our learners grow into God-fearing, socially responsible citizens.

To our students: Dream big, work hard, and stay disciplined. Your future is built on the choices you make today. Remember that excellence is not an act, but a habit.

— The Principal

To our parents and guardians: Thank you for the tremendous trust you have placed in us by entrusting us with your most precious gift — your children. We do not take this responsibility lightly. We promise to walk alongside you in partnership, maintaining open communication and working collaboratively to raise responsible, resilient, and resourceful citizens.

To our teaching and non-teaching staff: Your dedication, professionalism, and love for our students form the very heartbeat of this school. You are the architects of our success story. Let us continue to inspire, mentor, and lead by example, knowing that the seeds we sow today will bear fruit for generations to come.

To our Board of Management, the PTA, and our wider community: Your unwavering support, counsel, and resources enable us to pursue excellence without compromise. Together, we form a formidable team committed to one noble cause — the holistic development of every child under our care.

As we look to the future, our strategic vision remains crystal clear: to raise scholars, leaders, innovators, and patriots who will make a positive, transformative difference in Kenya and across the world. We are committed to expanding our infrastructure, enhancing our academic programmes, strengthening our co-curricular offerings, and deepening our spiritual foundation.

We are excited to welcome our pioneer Grade 10 students as we fully embrace the Competency-Based Education curriculum. This is a new chapter filled with promise, and we are fully prepared to deliver an education that is learner-centred, skills-focused, and values-driven.

Once again, welcome to The Devki Ruiru Senior School. Whether you are a prospective student, a parent exploring options, a partner seeking collaboration, or a well-wisher visiting our website — you are part of our story. Together, we build excellence. Together, we ignite futures.

God bless you, and God bless The Devki Ruiru Senior School.

The Principal

The Principal's Core Commitments

Five foundational commitments that guide every decision and initiative at Devki Ruiru.

📚

Academic Rigor

Maintaining the highest standards of teaching, learning, and assessment to ensure every student achieves their full intellectual potential.

🌍

Character Formation

Building integrity, discipline, and moral courage in every learner through daily example, mentorship, and values-based education.

💡

Innovation & Skills

Embracing the CBE curriculum and modern technology to prepare students for careers that do not yet exist in our rapidly changing world.

🤝

Community Partnership

Fostering strong collaboration between parents, teachers, and the wider community to create a supportive ecosystem for every child.

🕊

Spiritual Growth

Nurturing God-fearing citizens who lead with compassion, serve with humility, and live with purpose and faith.

🏆

Excellence in All

Pursuing distinction not only in academics but in sports, arts, leadership, and service to humanity.
